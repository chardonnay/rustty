== Upstream Attribution

RusTTY is designed to interoperate with and, over time, reimplement behavior
from PuTTY. Any code, fixtures, formats, or adapted material derived from PuTTY
must preserve the relevant PuTTY attribution and permission notice.

The project-wide attribution record lives at xref:../../NOTICE.adoc[NOTICE.adoc].

== Working Rule

When adding material derived from PuTTY:

* keep the upstream notice intact,
* document provenance in the affected file or nearby docs,
* mention the compatibility work in changelogs and release notes, and
* avoid presenting derived material as wholly original RusTTY work.
